# Quill — The Keeper of the Words

Owl-elder NPC for the `the-word` phase (US-W2). Stands at the Briar Wilds threshold
and gives Pip the Word as a free gift (sets `has_word`, chains the `word-given` scene,
then despawns like the Keeper). Allegory governed by `biblical-guidance.md`; the in-game
story stays allegorical and never names Jesus (explicit doctrine lives in the credits).

## Sprite (PixelLab v3, character `0825bc06-5dcb-4c4e-a299-34af1f319471`, 8 directions, 68×68 canvas, low top-down)

wise gentle anthropomorphic owl elder, humanoid owl, soft warm amber and tawny-brown
feathers, rounded facial disc, small ear tufts, large calm golden eyes, short hooked
beak, simple earth-toned robe, kind patient teacherly bearing, watercolor storybook
palette, soft painterly textures, children's-book illustration style. 3/4-oblique
top-down (top + one front face), Pip-scale (~48px character in 68px canvas — matches
marsh-hermit / old-man).

Animations (v3 custom, 4 frames each, frames 000-003 of the 5-frame export — 004 is the
loop-close): `idle` ("standing still, breathing gently, a calm idle"), `walk` ("walking").
